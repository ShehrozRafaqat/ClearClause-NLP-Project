CREATOR & SOCIAL MEDIA TALENT AGREEMENT

This Creator & Social Media Talent Agreement is entered into as of July 1, 2026 between Citrus Beverages, Inc., a New York corporation ("Brand"), and the social media talent who signs the order form ("Talent").

A. Deliverables and Approval Cycle
Talent shall produce the social media content described on the order form, including feed posts, stories, and short-form video, on the schedule set out there. Brand may request revisions to any submission and will provide approval or revision notes within five business days. Talent agrees to provide reasonable revisions at no additional fee until Brand is satisfied that the content reflects Brand voice.

B. Compensation
Brand will pay Talent the campaign fee listed on the order form. Fifty percent is invoiced upon contract execution and the remainder upon Brand's final approval of all deliverables. Invoices are payable Net 45. Brand may dispute and withhold payment for any submission that is rejected for not meeting the Brand voice guidelines.

C. Content Ownership and Usage
Talent retains authorship of the creative work but grants Brand a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, and sublicense the deliverables across any media now known or later invented. Talent assigns all rights, title, and interest in any custom hashtags, slogans, or campaign concepts created during the engagement to Brand.

D. Category Exclusivity
For the duration of the campaign and for ninety days after the final deliverable is published, Talent shall not promote, endorse, or appear in paid content for any competing beverage, energy drink, or hydration product. Brand will provide an updated list of named competitors on request.

E. Confidentiality and Pre-Release Embargo
Talent shall keep all creative briefs, campaign timing, product launch details, and other Brand information confidential during the campaign and for two years after final delivery. Information that is publicly available, already known, or independently developed is not confidential under this agreement.

F. FTC Compliance and Brand Safety
Talent shall comply with all applicable disclosure rules, including the FTC Endorsement Guides, and shall clearly mark sponsored content with the appropriate disclosures. Talent represents that the content does not infringe any third-party rights and is suitable for Brand's family-friendly audience.

G. Brand Indemnity
Talent shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Brand and its affiliates from and against any and all third-party claims, losses, damages, costs, and attorneys' fees arising out of Talent's content, breach of the FTC rules, or violation of any third-party rights.

H. Termination
Brand may terminate this Agreement at its sole discretion, for any reason or no reason, on seven days written notice. Talent may terminate only for Brand's material breach if Brand fails to cure the breach within thirty days of written notice. Upon termination, Talent shall deliver any work in progress to Brand.

I. Limits on Brand's Liability
In no event shall Brand be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, or lost profit damages. Brand's total cumulative liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid to Talent in the three months preceding the claim.

J. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be settled by binding arbitration administered by JAMS in New York County, New York. The parties waive any right to trial by jury.

K. Talent Conduct
Talent shall maintain a public image consistent with Brand's family-friendly values throughout the campaign. Brand may, in its sole judgment, deem any post or public statement by Talent to be inconsistent with Brand values and require its removal at Talent's expense.

L. Effective Date
This Agreement is effective on the date the order form is signed by both parties and continues until all deliverables on the order form are accepted and paid for, unless terminated earlier under Section H.
