MASTER SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master SaaS Subscription Agreement is entered into as of June 15, 2026 between PivotCloud Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation, and the Customer that accepts these terms.

1. Subscription and Scope of Services
Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable subscription to access and use the PivotCloud platform during the Subscription Term, solely for Customer's internal business purposes and subject to the order form and acceptable use policy.

2. Fees and Payment
Customer shall pay the annual subscription fee in advance, invoiced upon signature. All invoices are payable Net 30. Provider may dispute any invoice within 30 days and may suspend access to the platform if Customer fails to pay any undisputed amount within ten days of written notice.

3. Term and Automatic Renewal
The initial term is twelve months from the Effective Date. The Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term. Renewal fees may be adjusted by up to 7 percent per year.

4. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days of written notice. Provider may terminate or suspend the Customer's access for any reason or no reason, at its sole discretion, upon 14 days written notice.

5. Intellectual Property
Provider retains all right, title, and interest in the PivotCloud platform and all underlying technology, including any improvements, derivative works, or feedback provided by Customer. Customer irrevocably assigns to Provider all rights in any feedback, suggestions, or ideas regarding the platform.

6. Customer Data
Customer retains ownership of Customer Data. Customer grants Provider a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable license to use Customer Data in aggregated and anonymised form for service improvement, benchmarking, and research purposes. Provider may retain Customer Data for thirty days after termination for backup purposes.

7. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect the other party's confidential information using reasonable safeguards for a period of five years after termination. Confidential information does not include information that is publicly available, already known, independently developed, or required by law to be disclosed.

8. Data Privacy and Security
Provider shall implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data. Customer is responsible for obtaining all necessary consents. Customer shall indemnify Provider against any claims arising from Customer's failure to comply with applicable privacy laws.

9. Indemnification
Customer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider, its affiliates, officers, directors, and employees from and against any and all third-party claims, losses, damages, costs, expenses, and attorneys' fees arising out of Customer's use of the platform, breach of this Agreement, or violation of applicable law.

10. Limitation of Liability
In no event shall Provider be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, punitive, or lost profit damages. Provider's total cumulative liability shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the three months preceding the claim. Customer's liability for confidentiality, indemnification, and breach of acceptable use shall be unlimited.

11. Service Levels
Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain platform availability of 99.5 percent measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance and force majeure events. Customer's exclusive remedy for any service level failure is a service credit not exceeding 10 percent of the monthly fee.

12. Non-Solicitation
For 24 months after termination, Customer shall not directly or indirectly solicit or hire any employee of Provider with whom Customer had material contact during the Subscription Term.

13. Assignment and Change of Control
Customer may not assign this Agreement without Provider's prior written consent. Provider may assign this Agreement to any affiliate, successor, or acquirer without notice to Customer.

14. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be settled by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association in Wilmington, Delaware. The parties waive any right to a jury trial or class action.

15. Effective Date
This Agreement is effective as of the date Customer accepts the order form or first accesses the platform, whichever occurs earlier.
