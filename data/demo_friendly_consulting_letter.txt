FRIENDLY CONSULTING LETTER OF ENGAGEMENT

This short letter of engagement is between BlueBird Studio Co-op and an independent contractor. It is intended to be readable in one sitting and to be fair to both sides.

1. Scope and Acceptance
The contractor will provide the services described in a one-page Statement of Work attached to this letter. The Statement of Work lists specific, testable acceptance criteria, the included revisions per deliverable, and the dates of each milestone. Anything outside the Statement of Work is handled through a written change request with additional fees agreed in advance.

2. Payment
BlueBird Studio Co-op will pay a thirty percent advance before work begins and the remainder upon final acceptance of each milestone. Invoices are payable Net 15 from the invoice date. If BlueBird Studio Co-op questions any line item in good faith, it will pay all undisputed amounts on schedule and resolve the questioned line within ten business days.

3. Each Party Keeps What It Already Owns
Each party retains ownership of its own pre-existing tools, templates, know-how, and reusable code libraries. After full payment, the contractor grants BlueBird Studio Co-op a license to use the final deliverables for its own business. The contractor may show non-confidential portions of the work in a portfolio with the studio's reasonable approval.

4. Mutual Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other party's confidential information using reasonable safeguards for a period of two years after the engagement ends. The usual exceptions apply: information that is publicly available, already known, independently developed, or required by law to be disclosed is not confidential under this letter.

5. Mutual Termination
Either party may end this engagement on thirty days written notice. BlueBird Studio Co-op will pay for all work performed up to the termination date and for any approved expenses already committed. There are no penalties, kill fees, or restrictions on either party after termination.

6. Liability Is Mutual and Capped
Each party's total liability under this engagement shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve months before any claim. Neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, except in the limited case of wilful misconduct. The cap is mutual and applies symmetrically to both sides.

7. Governing Law
This letter is governed by mutual agreement under the laws of the country where the contractor resides. If a question comes up, the parties will first try to work it out through good-faith negotiation between the named contacts on each side.

8. Effective Date and Term
This letter becomes effective on the date both parties sign and continues until the Statement of Work is complete. There is no automatic renewal. Any extension is agreed by a new signed Statement of Work.

Signed by BlueBird Studio Co-op and the contractor on the date written above. Each party keeps a copy and may make reasonable copies for its own records.
